About the Loan Payment Calculator

This calculator helps you estimate the payment required to repay a loan based on the loan amount, interest rate, loan term, and payment frequency.

Loan Payment Tip

A lower interest rate can significantly reduce the total interest paid over the life of a loan. Compare loan terms carefully because a longer term may reduce your payment but increase your total interest cost.
