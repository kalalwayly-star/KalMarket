This calculator estimates gross income, CPP, CPP2, EI, federal income tax, provincial income tax, and take-home pay for employees in Canada.

Paycheck Calculator Tip

Your take-home pay is different from your gross pay because payroll deductions such as CPP, EI, and income tax are deducted before you receive your net pay.
