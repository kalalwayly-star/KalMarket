About the Cash Flow Projection Calculator

This calculator helps business owners estimate their cash position over the next 12 months by projecting sales, income, expenses, loan payments, monthly cash flow, and ending cash.

Why Cash Flow Matters

A business can be profitable and still experience a cash shortage. A monthly cash flow projection helps you identify potential cash shortages before they happen.
