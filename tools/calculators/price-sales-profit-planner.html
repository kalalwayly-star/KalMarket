6. Pricing Strategy Guide

💵 Cost-Plus

Calculate your cost and add a desired markup. Simple and useful for many products and services.

🎯 Target Profit

Set the profit you want to earn and calculate the price or sales volume needed to reach it.

🏪 Competitive

Compare your price with competitors while considering your costs, quality and customer expectations.

💎 Value-Based

Price according to the value your product or service provides to the customer.

📦 Volume

Use a lower price to encourage higher sales volume and increase total revenue or profit.

👑 Premium

Charge a higher price by positioning your product or service as premium or specialized.

💡 Important

Higher sales revenue does not always mean higher profit.
