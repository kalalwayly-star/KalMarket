Canadian Resume Guide

Learn how to create a professional Canadian-style resume that helps employers quickly understand your experience, skills and qualifications.

⭐ Quick Tip

Your resume should be clear, easy to read and focused on the job you are applying for. You usually do not need to include your photo, age, marital status or other personal information.

What Is a Canadian Resume?

A Canadian resume is a short document that summarizes your work experience, education, skills and qualifications. Employers use it to decide whether they want to interview you for a position.

Main Resume Sections

📞 Contact Information

Include your name, phone number, professional email address and city/province.

👤 Professional Summary

Write 2–4 sentences describing your experience, strongest skills and the type of position you want.

💼 Work Experience

List your most relevant jobs, responsibilities, achievements and dates of employment.

🛠️ Skills

Include technical skills, workplace skills, languages and other abilities relevant to the job.

🎓 Education

List your degrees, diplomas, certificates and relevant training.

📜 Certifications

Add professional licenses, certificates and industry training when they are relevant.

❌ What to Avoid

  • Do not include your age or date of birth.
  • Do not include your marital status.
  • Do not include your SIN or other sensitive identification numbers.
  • Avoid unnecessary personal information.
  • Avoid spelling and grammar mistakes.
  • Do not send the exact same resume for every job.

How to Describe Your Work Experience

Focus on what you accomplished, not only on the duties you performed. Whenever possible, use numbers to show your results.

❌ Weak Example

Responsible for serving customers and handling cash.

✅ Stronger Example

Served an average of 80 customers per day, processed cash and card payments accurately, and helped maintain high customer satisfaction.

🌎 Tips for Newcomers to Canada

  • Your experience from another country can still be valuable.
  • Clearly explain transferable skills.
  • Mention Canadian certificates or training you have completed.
  • Include language skills when they are relevant to the job.
  • Consider adjusting your resume for each position.

📄 How Long Should Your Resume Be?

For many jobs, one or two pages is enough. Focus on your most relevant experience rather than including every job you have ever had.

✅ Resume Before You Apply

  • My contact information is correct.
  • My resume is easy to read.
  • My experience matches the job.
  • I checked spelling and grammar.
  • I removed unnecessary personal information.
  • I saved the final version as a PDF when appropriate.
