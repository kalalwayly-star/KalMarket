🧼 Start a Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning Business

Carpet and upholstery cleaning can be a profitable service business because homes, apartments, offices, vehicles, and commercial properties regularly need professional cleaning.

You can start with a small amount of equipment, work independently, build a local customer base, and gradually expand into larger residential and commercial contracts.

1. Choose Your Cleaning Services

Decide which cleaning services you want to offer. Starting with a few core services can make it easier to control your equipment costs and build experience.

  • 🧼 Residential Carpet Cleaning
  • 🛋️ Upholstery Cleaning
  • 🧹 Area Rug Cleaning
  • 🪜 Stair Carpet Cleaning
  • 🛏️ Mattress Cleaning
  • 🚗 Vehicle Interior Cleaning
  • 🏢 Commercial Carpet Cleaning

2. Understand Your Startup Costs

Startup costs depend on whether you purchase new or used equipment and whether you operate from a vehicle, home, or commercial location.

  • Carpet cleaning machine or extractor
  • Professional vacuum
  • Cleaning chemicals and supplies
  • Vehicle or transportation
  • Business insurance
  • Business registration and licenses
  • Advertising and marketing

3. Equipment & Supplies

Professional equipment can improve cleaning quality and allow you to complete jobs more efficiently.

  • Carpet extractor
  • Commercial vacuum
  • Carpet cleaning wand
  • Spot cleaning equipment
  • Cleaning brushes
  • Carpet and upholstery cleaning chemicals
  • Gloves and protective equipment
  • Air movers or drying equipment
💡 Start With the Essentials

Start with equipment that matches the services you already have customers for. Upgrade as your business grows.

4. Set Your Prices

Your pricing should cover labor, cleaning products, transportation, equipment costs, insurance, overhead, and profit.

💡 Simple Pricing Formula

Service Price = Labor + Materials + Travel + Overhead + Profit

Depending on the service, you may charge by the room, square foot, item, hour, or complete job.

💡 Example: How to Calculate Your Price

Here is a simple example showing how a business can calculate the price of a service.

Labor $90
Materials $15
Travel $10
Overhead $15
Total Cost $130
Desired Profit $40
Customer Price $170

Customer Price = Total Cost + Desired Profit

$130 + $40 = $170

5. Calculate Your Profit

Revenue is only one part of your business. You need to subtract your operating costs to understand your actual profit.

  • Total customer revenue
  • Employee or contractor costs
  • Cleaning chemicals and supplies
  • Vehicle and fuel costs
  • Equipment maintenance
  • Insurance
  • Marketing
  • Other operating expenses

6. Get Your First Customers

Your first customers can come from your local community, referrals, property managers, real estate professionals, and online listings.

  • Post your services on local marketplaces
  • Use social media
  • Ask satisfied customers for referrals
  • Build positive reviews
  • Contact real estate professionals
  • Contact property managers

7. Marketing Your Business

Carpet and upholstery cleaning is highly visual, which makes before-and-after results especially useful for marketing.

  • Take before-and-after photos
  • Create a professional business name and logo
  • Keep your vehicle clean and professional
  • Create an online presence
  • Encourage customer reviews

8. Insurance & Business Requirements

Depending on your location and services, you may need business registration, licenses, permits, insurance, or other requirements.

⚠️ Important

Requirements vary by location and service. Check the applicable government requirements and insurance needs before starting your business.

9. Hiring Employees

You can start by working alone and hire additional cleaners as your customer base grows.

Before hiring, understand payroll, employment requirements, insurance, training, and workplace safety obligations.

10. Grow Your Business

Once you have consistent customers, you can grow by adding services, expanding your service area, hiring employees, and targeting commercial customers.

  • Add additional cleaning services
  • Expand your service area
  • Hire additional cleaners
  • Invest in professional equipment
  • Target commercial customers
  • Build recurring cleaning contracts

✅ Carpet Cleaning Business Startup Checklist

  • Choose your cleaning services
  • Research local demand
  • Calculate startup costs
  • Purchase necessary equipment
  • Determine your pricing
  • Check licenses and requirements
  • Obtain appropriate insurance
  • Create your business brand
  • Start marketing
  • Get your first customers
  • Track revenue and expenses
  • Build recurring customers
  • Reinvest and grow
