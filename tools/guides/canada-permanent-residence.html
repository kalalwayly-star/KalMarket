How to Become a Permanent Resident of Canada

Learn about Canada's permanent residence pathways, basic requirements, application steps, and where to find official information.

⚠️ Important Information

Canadian immigration programs have different eligibility requirements. Rules, fees, programs, and selection criteria can change. This guide provides general educational information and is not legal immigration advice. Always verify the current requirements with the Government of Canada before applying.

What Is Permanent Residence?

Permanent residence allows eligible foreign nationals to live in Canada permanently and generally gives them the right to work and study in Canada, subject to Canadian laws and the conditions of their status.

A permanent resident is not the same as a Canadian citizen. Permanent residents have permanent resident status but remain citizens of another country unless they later become Canadian citizens.

Main Ways to Become a Permanent Resident

Canada has several permanent residence programs. The right pathway depends on your education, work experience, language ability, family situation, province, and other factors.

🚀

Express Entry

Express Entry is an online system used to manage applications from skilled immigrants.

  • Canadian Experience Class
  • Federal Skilled Worker Program
  • Federal Skilled Trades Program
📍

Provincial Nominee Programs

Provinces and territories can nominate eligible people who meet the requirements of their immigration streams.

Some PNP pathways work through Express Entry, while others use a separate application process.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Sponsorship

Canadian citizens and permanent residents may be able to sponsor eligible family members to become permanent residents.

Programs include sponsorship of spouses, partners, dependent children, and certain relatives.

🌎

Other Immigration Programs

Canada also has other permanent residence programs for specific groups, occupations, regions, and circumstances.

Check the official Government of Canada website to find programs that may apply to your situation.

🚀 How Express Entry Works

  1. Check whether you meet the eligibility requirements of an Express Entry program.
  2. Prepare your required documents, including language test results and education documents when applicable.
  3. Create your Express Entry profile and enter the pool if you are eligible.
  4. Your profile is scored and ranked using the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS).
  5. If you receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA), you can submit your permanent residence application.
  6. Submit your application, documents, and required fees within the applicable deadline.
  7. Complete biometrics and any other requested steps.
  8. IRCC processes your application and makes a decision.

📄 Documents You May Need

The documents you need depend on the immigration program and your personal circumstances.

  • Valid passport or travel document
  • Approved language test results, when required
  • Education documents and credential assessment, when required
  • Work experience documents
  • Police certificates, when required
  • Proof of funds, when required
  • Medical examination, when required
  • Family and civil-status documents, when applicable

Do not assume that every applicant needs every document. Follow the personalized document checklist provided by IRCC for your application.

📝 General Permanent Residence Application Process

  1. Find the immigration program that matches your situation.
  2. Review the eligibility requirements carefully.
  3. Prepare your documents and complete any required language, education, or other assessments.
  4. Create a profile or submit a nomination application, depending on the program.
  5. Receive an invitation or nomination if required by your program.
  6. Complete and submit your permanent residence application.
  7. Pay the required government fees.
  8. Provide biometrics and complete other requested requirements.
  9. Wait for IRCC to process your application.
  10. If approved, follow the instructions to confirm your permanent resident status and prepare to settle in Canada.

⏳ Processing Times

Processing times vary depending on the immigration program, the type of application, whether the application is complete, and other factors.

Processing times can change. Always check the current processing time on the official Canada.ca website rather than relying on an old estimate.

🇨🇦 If Your Application Is Approved

If your permanent residence application is approved, IRCC will provide instructions and documents needed to confirm your permanent resident status and, when applicable, travel to Canada.

Depending on your situation, you may receive a Confirmation of Permanent Residence (COPR) and, if required, a permanent resident visa.

Before travelling, carefully check the information on your documents and follow the instructions provided by IRCC.

🇨🇦 Official Government of Canada Resources

Use the official Government of Canada website to check eligibility, program requirements, current fees, processing times, application instructions, and other important information.
