Canada Immigration & Temporary Stay Options

🏠 Permanent Residence

Permanent residence allows eligible immigrants to live and work in Canada on a long-term basis. Canada has several immigration programs, and eligibility depends on your personal situation.

✈️ Visit Canada

Depending on your citizenship, travel document, and circumstances, you may need a visitor visa or an electronic travel authorization (eTA).

💼 Work in Canada

Most foreign nationals need a work permit to work in Canada. The two main types are employer-specific work permits and open work permits.

🎓 Study in Canada

International students may need a study permit. The process can involve choosing a designated learning institution, receiving an acceptance letter, and meeting other requirements.

🇨🇦 Official Government of Canada Resources

Before submitting an immigration, visitor, work, or study application, always verify the current requirements directly with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
