🚚 Start a Moving & Delivery Business

Moving and local delivery services can be attractive businesses because customers and local businesses regularly need help transporting furniture, appliances, packages, supplies, and other items.

You can start small with one vehicle and gradually grow by adding customers, additional services, vehicles, and drivers.

1. Choose Your Service

The first step is deciding exactly what type of transportation service you want to provide.

  • 🏠 Residential Moving
  • 🛋️ Furniture Delivery
  • 🧊 Appliance Delivery
  • 🗑️ Junk Removal
  • 📦 Local Package Delivery
  • 🏢 Business-to-Business Delivery
  • 🚗 Local Courier Services

2. Understand Your Startup Costs

Your startup costs will depend on the type of service, vehicle, equipment, and service area you choose.

  • Vehicle purchase or financing
  • Commercial vehicle and business insurance
  • Moving equipment and tools
  • Fuel and transportation costs
  • Business registration and permits
  • Advertising and marketing
  • Scheduling and invoicing software

3. Vehicle & Equipment

Your vehicle is one of the most important investments in this type of business. Choose equipment based on the services you plan to offer.

  • Moving dollies
  • Tie-down straps
  • Moving blankets
  • Boxes and packing materials
  • Basic hand tools
💡 Start Small

Avoid buying expensive equipment before you have enough customers to justify the investment.

4. Set Your Prices

Pricing should cover your labor, fuel, vehicle costs, insurance, overhead, and desired profit.

💡 Simple Pricing Formula

Service Price = Labor + Fuel + Vehicle Costs + Materials + Overhead + Profit

5. Calculate Your Profit

Revenue is only one part of the business. You need to understand your expenses to determine your actual operating profit.

  • Total customer revenue
  • Fuel expenses
  • Vehicle payments and maintenance
  • Employee or driver costs
  • Insurance
  • Marketing
  • Other operating expenses

6. Find Your First Customers

Your first customers can come from your local community, businesses, referrals, and online advertising.

  • Post your services on local marketplaces
  • Use social media
  • Ask customers for referrals
  • Contact local businesses
  • Build relationships with real estate professionals

7. Marketing Your Business

Professional branding and reliable service can help a small transportation business build trust and generate repeat customers.

  • Create a professional business name and logo
  • Put your business information on your vehicle
  • Use professional photos
  • Build an online presence
  • Encourage customer reviews

8. Insurance & Business Requirements

Transportation businesses may have specific insurance, registration, licensing, vehicle, and safety requirements depending on the location and type of service.

⚠️ Important

Requirements vary by location and service. Check the applicable government and insurance requirements before starting operations.

9. Hiring Drivers

You can often start with one vehicle and operate the business yourself. As demand grows, additional drivers can help you accept more jobs.

Before hiring, understand payroll, employment requirements, insurance, driver qualifications, training, and workplace safety obligations.

10. Grow Your Business

Once you have consistent customers, you can grow by adding vehicles, expanding your service area, offering additional services, and hiring drivers.

  • Add additional vehicles
  • Expand your service area
  • Add complementary services
  • Hire additional drivers
  • Build recurring business customers

✅ Moving & Delivery Startup Checklist

  • Choose your service
  • Research local demand
  • Calculate startup costs
  • Choose an appropriate vehicle
  • Purchase necessary equipment
  • Determine your pricing
  • Check licenses and requirements
  • Obtain appropriate insurance
  • Create your business brand
  • Start marketing
  • Get your first customers
  • Track revenue and expenses
  • Reinvest and grow
