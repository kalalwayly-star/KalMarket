🧹 Start a Cleaning Business

A cleaning business can be started with relatively low startup costs and can serve both residential and commercial customers.

You can begin by working alone, build a customer base, and gradually expand by adding services, employees, and larger commercial accounts.

1. Choose Your Cleaning Service

Start by choosing the type of cleaning service you want to provide. You can specialize in one service or offer several related services.

  • 🏠 Residential House Cleaning
  • 🏢 Commercial Office Cleaning
  • 🧽 Deep Cleaning
  • 📦 Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning
  • 🪟 Window Cleaning
  • 🧼 Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning
  • 🏗️ Post-Construction Cleaning

2. Understand Your Startup Costs

One advantage of a cleaning business is that many services can be started without a large investment.

  • Cleaning supplies and chemicals
  • Vacuum cleaners and cleaning equipment
  • Vehicle or transportation
  • Business insurance
  • Business registration and licenses
  • Advertising and marketing
  • Scheduling and invoicing software

3. Equipment & Supplies

Start with the basic equipment required for your services. Avoid purchasing expensive equipment until your customer base justifies the investment.

  • Professional vacuum
  • Mops and buckets
  • Microfiber cloths
  • Cleaning products
  • Protective gloves
  • Garbage bags
  • Storage containers
💡 Start With the Basics

Buy the equipment you need for your first customers and upgrade as your business generates revenue.

4. Set Your Prices

Your prices should cover labor, supplies, transportation, insurance, overhead, and your desired profit.

💡 Simple Pricing Formula

Service Price = Labor + Supplies + Travel + Overhead + Profit

You can charge customers by the hour, by the job, by the room, or using a recurring service package.

5. Calculate Your Profit

Revenue does not equal profit. You need to subtract all business expenses to understand how much your business is actually earning.

  • Total customer revenue
  • Employee or contractor costs
  • Cleaning supplies
  • Vehicle and fuel costs
  • Insurance
  • Marketing
  • Other operating expenses

6. Get Your First Customers

Your first customers can come from your local community, referrals, online listings, and relationships with local businesses.

  • Post your services on local marketplaces
  • Use social media
  • Ask satisfied customers for referrals
  • Build positive reviews
  • Contact real estate professionals
  • Contact local businesses

7. Marketing Your Business

A professional image can help customers feel confident about allowing your business into their homes or workplaces.

  • Create a professional business name and logo
  • Keep your vehicle clean and professional
  • Take before-and-after photos
  • Create an online presence
  • Encourage customer reviews

8. Insurance & Business Requirements

Depending on your location and the services you provide, you may need business registration, permits, licenses, insurance, or other requirements.

⚠️ Important

Requirements vary by location and service. Check the applicable government requirements and insurance needs before starting your business.

9. Hiring Employees

You can often start a cleaning business by yourself. As demand increases, hiring cleaners can allow you to serve more customers.

Before hiring, make sure you understand payroll, employment requirements, insurance, training, and workplace safety obligations.

10. Grow Your Business

Once you have consistent customers, you can grow by adding services, expanding your service area, hiring employees, and targeting larger commercial accounts.

  • Add additional cleaning services
  • Expand your service area
  • Hire additional cleaners
  • Invest in professional equipment
  • Target commercial customers
  • Build recurring cleaning contracts

✅ Cleaning Business Startup Checklist

  • Choose your cleaning service
  • Research local demand
  • Calculate startup costs
  • Purchase necessary equipment
  • Determine your pricing
  • Check licenses and requirements
  • Obtain appropriate insurance
  • Create your business brand
  • Start marketing
  • Get your first customers
  • Track revenue and expenses
  • Build recurring customers
  • Reinvest and grow
