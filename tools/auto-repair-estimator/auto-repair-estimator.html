How the Auto Repair Estimator Works

Enter your vehicle information, select your repair type, and enter the expected labour, parts and additional costs. The calculator estimates the subtotal, applicable taxes and final repair cost.

Tax rates can be adjusted for your city, province, state or country. This makes the calculator useful for users in Canada and internationally.
